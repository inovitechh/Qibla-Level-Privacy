Privacy Policy — Qibla & Level

INOVI TECH • com.inovitech.qiblalevel • Last updated: November 2026

INOVI TECH built the Qibla & Level (com.inovitech.qiblalevel) mobile application as a utility tool. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information.

1. Location Information

Qibla & Level requests access to your device's location (GPS / Network) strictly on-device to:

  • Calculate accurate Qibla bearing and distance to the Kaaba.
  • Compute local geomagnetic declination (True North correction).
  • Display your position and Great Circle Qibla line on the map.

Your location data is processed locally on your device and is never collected, stored on external servers, sold, or shared with third parties by INOVI TECH. You may also use the app without granting location permission by selecting a city manually.

2. Device Sensors

The app reads your device's magnetometer, accelerometer, and gyroscope locally in real time to power the compass, Qibla needle, and 2D spirit level. Sensor data never leaves your device.

3. Map Tiles & Geocoding

When using the Map tab or online location search, the app connects to OpenStreetMap / Esri World Imagery tile servers and Android's Geocoder service to load map tiles and coordinates. No personal accounts or identifiers are transmitted.

4. Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Konum verileriniz yalnızca cihazınızda işlenir; INOVI TECH tarafından toplanmaz, sunucularda saklanmaz, satılmaz veya üçüncü taraflarla paylaşılmaz. Konum izni vermeden manuel şehir seçerek de uygulamayı kullanabilirsiniz.

2. Cihaz Sensörleri
